How it's built

Six stages, one journey — every stage reads what the previous one produced.

01 →

Lakeflow

Auto Loader ingests raw CSV/JSON from a Unity Catalog volume into bronze → silver → gold, with data-quality expectations.

02 →

Unity Catalog

PHI tags, column masks on name and phone, business comments and automatic lineage.

03 →

ML

Gradient-boosted readmission model, tracked in MLflow, registered in UC as champion, scores every patient.

04 →

Lakebase

Risk worklist and notes synced from UC into Postgres, plus a writable care_actions table.

05 →

Genie

Natural-language questions over the same governed gold tables.

06

Databricks App

Worklist, AI explanation (Claude via a serving endpoint), approve → write back to Lakebase.

One platform, one permission model — raw file, model score, served row and approved action are all governed by Unity Catalog.

Data & governance

Bad data is stopped at ingest, and patient identity never leaks.

Lakeflow pipeline
  • 2,000 patients · 6,038 raw discharges · 3,000 coordinator notes
  • 25 invalid rows dropped by the valid_length_of_stay expectation
  • 6,013 clean discharges in gold, ready for the model
  • Serverless, declarative, incremental (Auto Loader)
Unity Catalog
  • Name, phone and ZIP tagged phi
  • Name and phone masked for anyone outside the care team
  • Business comments on the risk table (also improves Genie)
  • Lineage captured bronze → silver → gold automatically

All data generated synthetically with a fixed seed — no real patients, no customer data.
